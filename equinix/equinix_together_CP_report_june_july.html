Executive Summary · July 2026

  • Traffic fell month-over-month — 252 total sessions vs 429 in June (-41%), a typical post-launch cooldown pattern.

Combined read: The consistent pattern across both months is a paradox worth surfacing to the Equinix team — engagement rates are high and improving, but avg. engagement time is falling. This typically means the audience is becoming more self-selecting (only genuinely interested visitors arrive), but they're spending less time per visit. Whether that's efficiency or drop-off requires scroll-depth data to resolve.

Recommendations

01 — Action
Set up scroll-depth tracking
The drop in avg. engagement time (−49% on both pages June→July) is the single most important open question. GA4 scroll-depth events will reveal whether visitors are reading more efficiently or leaving earlier. Requires a dedicated GA4 property setup before implementation.
02 — Monitor
Track August to confirm trend direction
Two months establishes a direction, not a trend. Pull August data and focus on three signals: does engagement rate continue rising? Does avg. engagement time stabilize or keep falling? Does returning user share grow month-over-month?
