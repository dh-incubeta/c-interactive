Why now

AI answers keep citing YouTube, and they rarely cite our clients' videos

We've seen the same pattern in three very different accounts. Clients treat YouTube as a paid awareness channel. AI engines treat it as a source of answers.

What clients believe

"YouTube is a media line, and ranking in search means we're found."

Video is bought for reach. The organic channel is a library of ads. Search rank is the visibility KPI.

What the data shows

"You rank, but you're not the answer."

AI answers pull from long-form, question-shaped videos. A brand can rank on page one and still be missing from the answer, while a competitor's or a creator's video gets the citation.

1 · What opens the door

Many doors, one primary: the AI Visibility Score

We can run it on public data before a contract exists, and it lands a finding no media plan can answer: AI is citing someone else's videos for your buyers' questions. Every door leads to the same first paid engagement.

Primary door

AI Visibility Score and YouTube citation read

Share of answer across six engines. Three answer from memory (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) and three search the web (Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode). Questions come from StoryVesting Bow Tie Funnel Behavioral Insights Matrix, not a keyword list. Each answer is scored absent, ghost-cited, mentioned or recommended. The YouTube read lists every video AI cites and whose channel it sits on.

Google referral

Google brings us in when an advertiser qualifies for funded creative (Creative Works, the Media Readiness Program). biBerk's production is Google-funded.

Incubeta US media clients

Accounts already buying YouTube with no organic or AI-answer layer. The score shows what their media spend isn't buying.

Channel audit

For brands that know their channel is weak: an audit of metadata, playlists, captions and links, set against the videos AI cites.

Webinars and roadshows

"You rank, but you're not the answer" as the session. Every attendee is offered a score.

How a score becomes a meeting, then a proposal

  1. Run itPublic data only. No client systems, no contract.
  2. ResampleAsk any headline question 3 to 5 times. Single runs flip about 1 in 5 times.
  3. Read out"You rank, but you're not the answer." Show the videos AI cites, and that none are theirs.
  4. Validate in their dataSearch Console AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions, plus Bing citations. This removes the "third-party proxy" objection.
  5. Propose the proofThe 120-day proof of concept, with two staked outcomes and a client owner for each.

The score opens the door. It's never the KPI. A single blended score hides the fact that most AI citations never name the brand they cite: 61.7% of them in a 2026 Semrush study. A citation can even feed a competitor's recommendation. We sell against the recommendation rate, reported by engine group, with sample counts and dates.

2 · How we get to a paid engagement

The 120-day proof of concept, shaped by biBerk

Six workstreams that prove cause, not just activity. The client sees a baseline, a prioritized fix list, a rebuilt channel, the first new videos and a controlled test, all in four months.

01Answer poll

100 unbranded buyer questions plus brand and current-customer checks. Each is asked 3 times on 7 engines. Polls 1 to 3 set the baseline, then we poll every two weeks to Day 120.

biBerk: 2,090 of 2,100 discovery answers collected, at about $63 a poll.

02Gap read

Every question is classed A (no page), B (page exists, not cited), C (cited, a competitor leads) or D (client leads), then ranked by demand.

biBerk: 55 questions already had a page AI passes over. Only 7 needed new pages.

03Channel fix

Playlists organized by question, titles rewritten as answers, one description template (the answer, a tracked link, FAQ), captions, chapters and a thumbnail system tested in YouTube.

biBerk: 7 playlists, 43 retitles, one Legal-approved template, five thumbnail templates.

04First new videos (Batch A)

Net-new videos only for questions where AI cites someone else's video. Each spec carries the question, evidence, audience, key points, placement, metadata, Legal flags and a success measure.

biBerk: Batch A covers 6 of the 58 YouTube questions.

05Hold-out test

Some playlists get the changes and the rest stay as they are, so movement can be credited to the work and not to the season or to other teams.

biBerk: two playlists change, three hold until poll 3 (Oct 28).

06Two Outcome Cards

Two staked outcomes with a named client owner each. The target is set after poll 3, not before, so it rests on a real baseline.

biBerk: (1) be the insurer AI names when owners choose and buy; (2) AI tells biBerk's side on reputation questions.
