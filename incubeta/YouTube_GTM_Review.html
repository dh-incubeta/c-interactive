Why now

AI answers keep citing YouTube, and they rarely cite our clients' videos

We've seen the same pattern in three very different accounts. Clients treat YouTube as a paid awareness channel. AI engines treat it as a source of answers.

What clients believe

"YouTube is a media line, and ranking in search means we're found."

Video is bought for reach. The organic channel is a library of ads. Search rank is the visibility KPI.

What the data shows

"You rank, but you're not the answer."

AI answers pull from long-form, question-shaped videos. A brand can rank on page one and still be missing from the answer, while a competitor's or a creator's video gets the citation.

How a score becomes a meeting, then a proposal

Step 2 · Step Inside

The first paid engagement, shaped by biBerk

Six workstreams that prove cause, not just activity. In four months the client sees a baseline, a ranked fix list, a rebuilt channel, the first new videos and a controlled test.

01Answer poll

100 unbranded buyer questions plus brand and current-customer checks. Each is asked 3 times on 7 engines. Polls 1 to 3 set the baseline, then we poll every two weeks to Day 120.

biBerk: 2,090 of 2,100 discovery answers collected, at about $63 a poll.

02Gap read

Every question is classed A (no page), B (page exists, not cited), C (cited, a competitor leads) or D (client leads), then ranked by demand.

biBerk: 55 questions already had a page AI passes over. Only 7 needed new pages.

03Channel fix

Playlists organized by question, titles rewritten as answers, one description template (the answer, a tracked link, FAQ), captions, chapters and a thumbnail system tested in YouTube.

biBerk: 7 playlists, 43 retitles, one Legal-approved template, five thumbnail templates.

04First new videos (Batch A)

Net-new videos only for questions where AI cites someone else's video. Each spec carries the question, evidence, audience, key points, placement, metadata, Legal flags and a success measure.

biBerk: Batch A covers 6 of the 58 YouTube questions.

05Hold-out test

Some playlists get the changes and the rest stay as they are, so movement can be credited to the work and not to the season or to other teams.

biBerk: two playlists change, three hold until poll 3 (Oct 28).

06Two Outcome Cards

Two staked outcomes with a named client owner each. The target is set after poll 3, not before, so it rests on a real baseline.

biBerk: (1) be the insurer AI names when owners choose and buy; (2) AI tells biBerk's side on reputation questions.

Our layer · what the ecosystem adds
  • Which questions to answer. The answer poll ranks the questions where AI cites someone else's video. No brief, no draft.
  • Why the audience acts. Audience insights and the Behavioral Insights Matrix set the stage and motive for each brief.
  • What paid should run. Organic winners, not guesses, become the 9+ asset sets the cheat sheet asks for.
  • What's working creatively. The CPI scores what performs. Our frameworks explain why.
