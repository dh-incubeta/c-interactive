What actually moves the needle

Ranked by strength of evidence, not by how good it sounds.

The uncomfortable finding: the levers with the strongest evidence are largely earned and operational, while the ones that sound most technical have the weakest support. Filter by tier to see what survives scrutiny.

Crawler access — the one lever with deterministic consequences

Everything else on this page is correlational. This table is not: the platforms document these behaviours themselves. Conflating the training crawler with the retrieval crawler is the most common and most expensive mistake in the category, and it is worth auditing on every partner site before anything else is discussed.

DecisionWhat it actually doesVerdict
Block GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended Opts out of model training only. No effect on whether you are cited or linked in AI answers. Low-cost IP position. Safe.
Block OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot Opts out of being cited and linked. OpenAI states the site "won't appear in ChatGPT search answers." Direct visibility loss.
Block Googlebot Leaves Google Search entirely, AI Overviews and AI Mode included. There is no partial opt-out of AI features while remaining in Search. Not a viable lever.
Block "AI crawlers" wholesale at the CDN The common misconfiguration: kills retrieval bots as collateral damage when the training-opt-out goal could have been met in robots.txt. Audit this first.
Rely on robots.txt alone Insufficient. User-triggered agents are documented as potentially ignoring it, and Cloudflare de-listed Perplexity in Aug 2025 over alleged Chrome-impersonating crawling. The edge is enforcement.

The strategic question

An answer engine's instinct is to skip the middle.

This is the part of the talk that matters most and is easiest to get wrong. Costco Services sits between a member and a provider. Every study of how AI answers select sources points the same direction: models name the endpoint and summarise away the layer in between. That is not a forecast — Google's May 2026 core update moved visibility in measured, paired swings.

Which sets up the honest version of the provider conversation. Making a provider more findable in general helps the provider. Making the Costco + provider answer the one that gets named — with member pricing, vetting and Executive benefits inside it — is the only version where provider growth and Costco's interest point the same way.

The counter-move: be a tool inside the assistant, not a page it summarises

The intermediaries holding their ground in 2026 all did the same thing — they exposed themselves as something the assistant can call, keeping the transaction, the loyalty layer and the data on their own rails. This is the most useful precedent set for a services marketplace, and it is a much more interesting close than a technical checklist.

Be precise about maturity — this is where credibility is won or lost. Agentic checkout is less settled than the coverage suggests. OpenAI deprioritised Instant Checkout in March 2026, telling merchants to use their own checkout experiences instead (Digital Commerce 360, Mar 6, 2026). Amazon won a preliminary injunction blocking Perplexity's shopping agent, now on appeal at the Ninth Circuit (CNBC, Mar 10, 2026) — so whether a user's own agent has a right to transact on a platform is genuinely unresolved. And roughly three-quarters of consumers say they will not let an agent autonomously complete a high-value purchase. Anyone still presenting ChatGPT Instant Checkout as the model of agentic commerce is about five months out of date.
The underweighted lever

YouTube is not a social channel here. It is an answer surface.

Of every factor tested across 75,000 brands, brand mentions in YouTube titles, transcripts and descriptions correlated highest with AI visibility — ahead of branded web mentions, and far ahead of domain authority and backlinks, which correlate at roughly zero. YouTube is also the single most-cited domain in Google AI Overviews. If the April deck treated YouTube as the broader vision, the evidence argues for promoting it to the main event.

Reach is irrelevant

View count correlates −0.03 with being cited. Subscribers, −0.03. Likes, −0.02. About 41% of cited videos had under 1,000 views (40.8% precisely) and 35% of the channels had under 10,000 subscribers. This is a structure game, not a scale game — which makes it available to any provider immediately.

Otterly, YouTube AI Citation Study 2026

Chapters are a Google-surface tactic

78% of timestamped videos were cited repeatedly, often across two to five different chapters — but timestamped citations appeared only in Google surfaces (73% AI Overviews, 27% AI Mode) and zero times in ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini or Perplexity. Precision matters: this is not a universal fix.

Otterly, 2026

Long-form wins

Long-form video accounts for 94% of YouTube's AI citations. Shorts, 5.7%. Playlists, channels and livestreams together, 0.3%. The asset that gets cited is a substantive, chaptered, well-described explainer — which is exactly what a service program needs anyway.

Otterly, 2026

Two honest caveats to carry on the slide. These are correlations on already-cited videos, so they explain repeat citation rather than initial eligibility, and no controlled study has shown that adding a transcript or chapters caused a citation. Separately, a widely-circulated claim that YouTube accounts for 23% of every AI Overview citation traces to a PR firm's aggregation of six vendors' datasets with no disclosed sample or geography. The defensible figure is 5.6% of all AI Overview citations (Ahrefs, 863,000 SERPs, March 2026). Use that one.
Measurement reality

What can be measured, and what is being sold as measurable.

A room of P&L owners will ask how this gets tracked, and the honest answer is more persuasive than an invented dashboard. Being straight about the limits here is what separates a subject-matter expert from a vendor.

Real and deterministic

Google Search Console — generative AI reports
Impressions for AI Overviews and AI Mode, broken out by page, country, device and date. Launched June 2026. But: no click data, AI Overviews and AI Mode are not separated, and it is still a phased rollout to a subset of sites. Confirm a partner actually has it before promising reporting on it.
Bing Webmaster Tools — AI Performance
Citations, page-level activity, and uniquely grounding queries — the phrases the AI actually used to retrieve. Nothing else exposes this. Covers Copilot, Bing and unnamed partner experiences; ChatGPT is not named.
Server and edge logs
Fully deterministic and badly underused. Which AI user-agents fetched which URLs, at what frequency, with what response codes. The only way to prove a site is not accidentally blocking retrieval bots.
Referral analytics
OpenAI appends utm_source=chatgpt.com, so those sessions are measurable in GA4. Set expectations honestly: AI referrals are still roughly 0.5% of sessions for ecommerce.

Estimated, and structurally so

Every third-party AI visibility tool works the same way: it runs prompts against public interfaces and counts appearances. None has platform-side data. That does not make them useless — it makes them a directional index rather than a KPI with a target.

"Be wary of third-party tools that promise ranking success or claim to use 'internal' Google metrics. No third-party tool has access to our internal ranking or AI systems."
Google, Guide to Optimizing for Generative AI Features · updated Jul 10, 2026
Non-determinism is the hard limit
There is less than a 1-in-100 chance that an AI tool returns the same list of brand recommendations on two runs of the same prompt, and roughly 1 in 1,000 for identical ordering. "Position three" is not a meaningful concept in an LLM answer.
Citations are not mentions
61.7% of AI citations never name the cited brand; 25.1% name a brand without citing it. Gemini names brands 83.7% of the time but cites only 21.4%; ChatGPT is the mirror image. A single blended "AI visibility score" destroys the only actionable signal.
Cross-platform scores average away the answer
Only 2.4% of cited URLs are shared by all three of ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews, and 91% appear in exactly one. Track citations and mentions separately, by engine.
The defensible stack to recommend from the stage: Search Console's generative AI report for impressions, Bing Webmaster Tools for citations and grounding queries, server logs to verify bot access, GA4 for referral outcomes — and one third-party prompt-monitoring tool, reported as a directional index with the variance stated out loud. Not five dashboards. One honest one.
Q&A prep

Fifteen minutes of questions is half the session.

The hardest questions in this room are the fair ones. Each answer below is short enough to say out loud and anchored to something checkable — including the two where the right answer is "we don't know yet."

Open decisions

What still needs deciding before the deck gets built.

Ranked by how much they change the shape of the talk rather than by effort.

1

Pick one narrative arc and commit

All three work. Mixing them is how a 30-minute talk to a mixed room loses the marketers to simplicity and the P&L owners to detail at the same time. This decision drives every other one.

2

Capture live AI answers for the four spotlight queries

Roughly an hour of work and the highest-leverage hour available. Screenshots of an actual AI Overview and ChatGPT reply — with the competitor named and Costco absent — do more than any chart on this page. Capture the same day as the talk; these answers change week to week.

3

Verify the shaky program facts, or cut them

Five of them. The CAP Shop Card amount, Protective's Executive-tier discount magnitude and American Family's Costco discount percentage are all unverifiable from public sources. Elavon's equipment-credit window closed July 31. And the CAP incentive dollar figures currently on the site are a Volkswagen model-specific offer expiring Aug 31, not a program-wide benefit. Wrong benefit detail in front of the partner who owns it is the worst possible error in this room.

4

Decide the position on FAQ work, explicitly

The FAQ programme is sound and worth continuing — but the reason has changed, and the room may include someone who knows Google killed FAQ rich results in May. Getting ahead of that is credibility; getting caught by it is not. Recommended framing: the content matches how members phrase questions and how models fan out queries; the markup is now inert.

5

Run a crawler-access audit on the four provider sites and the Costco Services pages

Two Costco Services program paths returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch on August 3. Whether AI retrieval bots get the same treatment is a one-afternoon check against server logs, and it either produces the most concrete finding in the deck or removes a claim that would not have held.

6

Settle what the room is asked to do

Education, not a pitch, still needs a next step or the energy dissipates in the parking lot. The lowest-pressure version that stays inside the brief: name the one thing each provider can check on their own site this week, and let anyone who wants more raise their hand.
