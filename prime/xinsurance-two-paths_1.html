01 · The starting point

xinsurance.com is a lead engine with a design on top

A ChatGPT mock-up shows what a page could look like. It can't show what the page has to do. Seven years of business rules sit beneath the design, and they are the part an AI tool can't see.

What AI tools do well today

  • Generate design directions and layouts in minutes
  • Draft and improve page copy, metadata and FAQs
  • Speed up coding, QA checklists and content migration
  • Analyze competitors, keyword gaps and AI-search visibility

What AI can't see on its own

  • How each risk-class button pre-fills the right quote form
  • How every form field maps into ClickDimensions and Dynamics 365
  • Which cookies fire, when, and under which consent choice
  • Which copy legal has already approved, and why it must stay

The answer isn't to avoid AI. It's to give AI the context and guardrails it needs, and that is the first step on either path.

    07 · How we work with AI

    AI builds the pages. People approve what goes live.

    AI-assisted content

    AI drafts copy, layouts and metadata. Your team and legal review it before it publishes.

    Approved building blocks

    AI works from a vetted set of components and tools, never from one-off code nobody has reviewed.

    Every change runs accessibility, consent, form and CRM checks before release.

    Clear accountability

    Compliance and legal liability rest on checked, documented work. That's why core architecture is never left to AI alone.
